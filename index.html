Parachute Fall – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 September 2026 · Last updated: 15 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Parachute Fall (the “Game”) handles information. The Game is developed and published by the developer named on the Game’s Google Play page (“we”, “us”, “our”). If you have any questions, contact us at stamgamesdev@gmail.com.

Internal information. The Game’s internal technical details, such as its technical identifiers and build information, are intentionally not published in this document. They are not personal data and do not affect your privacy. Here, the Game is identified by its name and by its Google Play page.

In short

  • We do not collect any personal data ourselves. The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device information such as your advertising ID (see section 8).
  • There are no accounts or sign-ins, no analytics or tracking tools of our own, and no in-app purchases.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your own device.
  • The Game needs an internet connection: to check that the connection works and to load ads. The connection checks contain no personal data.
  • This website uses no cookies. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Game shows no personalised ads. Where the law gives you a choice about personalised ads (for example, in some US states), you can make it in Settings → Ad choices.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal data through the Game ourselves. The Game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. The ad service built into the Game, Google AdMob, collects certain device information in order to show ads, as described in section 8.

2. Information stored only on your device

To remember your game, the Game saves a small file in its private storage on your device (Android “shared preferences”). It contains:

  • progress: unlocked levels and stars earned;
  • in-game items: gold, keys, abilities, and the parachutes and characters you have unlocked and equipped;
  • settings: sound, music, vibration, tilt control, graphics quality, control speed and language;
  • gameplay statistics, such as the number of levels completed and obstacles dodged;
  • the state of daily rewards and daily quests, whether the tutorial is finished and whether you have already been asked to rate the Game.

The Game never sends this file off your device, and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Gold and keys in the Game have no real-world value.

3. Device features the Game uses

The Game uses the following features only on your device, while you play. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere:

  • Device language: read at first start to show the Game in your language (you can change it in Settings).
  • Date and time: used to unlock daily rewards and daily quests.
  • Touch screen and, only if you turn on Tilt Control in Settings, the motion sensor (accelerometer), to steer the parachutist.
  • Vibration: short vibrations as feedback; you can turn them off in Settings.

4. Permissions

The Game does not ask for any permission that needs your approval (such as location, camera, microphone, contacts, files or phone). Its Android package contains only permissions that are granted automatically at installation:

  • INTERNET: for the internet connection the Game needs to be played (see below).
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: to see whether your device is connected to a network, so that the Game can tell you at once when the connection is lost.
  • VIBRATE: for the vibration feedback described above.
  • Permissions added by Google’s ad library: AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS let the ad service use your device’s advertising ID and Android’s privacy-focused ad features; WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE let it finish short background tasks, such as sending ad reports. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • An internal permission that Android’s own libraries use to stop other apps from sending messages to the Game. It gives the Game access to nothing.

The Game is played online and uses the internet for the following:

  • Connection check: while the Game is open, it downloads a tiny file (ping.txt) from our service on GitHub Pages (therooster0.github.io/parachute-fall-api) about every 30 seconds. If that file cannot be reached, it asks Google’s connection check (www.google.com/generate_204). Without a connection, the Game pauses until the connection is back.
  • Ads: the ad service loads ads and reports when they are shown or tapped (see section 8).

The connection-check requests contain no personal data and no account or device identifier. As with any internet connection, the servers that receive them (GitHub, and Google for the fallback check) see your IP address and basic technical information, such as the time of the request. Links you tap (for example, to rate the Game or to read this policy or our Terms of Use) open in Google Play or your web browser.

5. Third-party services

The Game contains one third-party SDK that processes data: Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), together with Google’s User Messaging Platform for consent. It contains no analytics, social network or payment SDKs. The following services may process information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (ads): provided by Google LLC (USA) and, in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, by Google Ireland Limited. See section 8, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play: when you download, install or update the Game, Google processes information as described in the Google Privacy Policy. Through Google Play Console, Google gives us only aggregated statistics (for example, the number of installs) and, if you have allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics data, anonymous crash and performance reports that do not identify you.
  • Rating the Game: if you choose to rate the Game, it opens its page in the Google Play Store; ratings and reviews you post there are handled by Google.
  • GitHub (our service): the connection check is hosted on GitHub Pages, provided by GitHub, Inc. (USA). When the Game connects, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address (GitHub Privacy Statement).
  • Google connection check: only if our service cannot be reached, the Game asks www.google.com/generate_204, which returns an empty answer; as with any website, Google sees your IP address (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Unity: the Game is made with the Unity engine by Unity Technologies. We have turned off Unity’s optional data services (Unity Analytics, hardware statistics, crash and performance reporting) and left Unity’s analytics module out of the Game entirely, so the Game sends no data to Unity. More information: Unity Privacy Policy.
  • Android backup: if backup is turned on on your device, Android may include the Game’s saved data in your device backup stored in your Google Account. This backup is controlled by you and Google; we have no access to it.
  • This page: this privacy policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open it, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address (GitHub Privacy Statement). We do not add any cookies, analytics or tracking to this page.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

We and this website use no cookies. The ad service in the Game (Google AdMob) uses your device’s advertising ID and similar identifiers to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, and, where this is allowed, to personalise them. Because the Game is meant for players from the age of 13, some of whom may be under the age of digital consent, all players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are treated as under that age: they are shown only non-personalised (limited) ads, and no consent form is shown. Where the law gives you a choice about personalised ads (for example, in some US states), you can make it at any time in Settings → Ad choices. Apart from this, the Game stores on your device only the save file described in section 2, which never leaves your device. This website (this policy and our Terms of Use) sets no cookies and loads nothing from other websites.

7. Sharing and selling of data

Because the Game itself collects no personal data, we have nothing to sell, rent, trade or share with anyone, including advertisers and data brokers. The services described in sections 4 and 5 (Google, GitHub) are based in the United States and may process technical data, such as IP addresses, there under their own safeguards.

The ad service (Google AdMob) receives the information described in section 8 directly from your device and processes it under its own responsibility and the Google Privacy Policy. From Google we receive only aggregated reports about the ads (for example, how many were shown), which do not identify you.

8. Advertising and in-app purchases

The Game is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob:

  • Between levels: a full-screen ad may appear when you leave the results of a finished level.
  • Rewarded ad (optional): when you run out of lives, you can choose to watch an ad to the end to get one more life instead of paying gold. If you close it early, no life is given.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect from your device: the advertising ID and other device identifiers; your approximate location, derived from your IP address; information about your device and the Game (such as device model, operating system, language and app version); your interactions with ads (such as views and taps); and crash and performance data of the ad library. If you have not given consent, or where the law requires it, Google shows only non-personalised or limited ads. We request only ads that are suitable for all ages (rated G). You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Nothing in the Game can currently be bought with real money. Gold and keys earned in the Game have no real-world value. If we add in-app purchases in the future, we will update this policy before they become available. For the rules on in-game items, purchases and refunds, see our Terms of Use.

9. Children’s privacy

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over, including teenagers, and is not directed at children under 13. Nobody is asked for personal data in the Game, ads are limited to content suitable for all ages, and in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland they are not personalised (see section 6). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the minimum age that applies in their country). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has sent us personal information (for example, by email), please contact us and we will delete it.

10. If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We use them only to reply to you, do not share them with anyone, and delete them when they are no longer needed to handle your request.

11. Data retention and deletion

  • Data on your device: stays until you uninstall the Game or clear its data (Android Settings → Apps → Parachute Fall → Storage → Clear data). This permanently resets your progress.
  • Emails: kept only as long as needed to reply to you. You can ask us to delete them at any time.
  • Ad data: kept by Google for its own retention periods (How Google retains data).

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) give you rights over your personal data, such as the right to access, correct, delete or port it, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw your consent. We ourselves hold only the emails you send us; to exercise your rights over them, write to stamgamesdev@gmail.com, and we will reply within 30 days. For ads, you can change or withdraw your consent at any time in Settings → Ad choices (where it is offered), and reset or delete your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your device settings; for data collected by Google’s ad service, you can contact Google directly. Under some US state laws, personalised ads may count as a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information; you can opt out of this by turning off personalised ads in your device settings. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority in your country.

13. Security

Your game data stays in the Game’s private storage on your device, protected by Android’s app sandbox, and is not sent over the internet. The Game’s connection check uses an encrypted HTTPS connection. Emails you send us are protected by the security measures of our email provider.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the Game gets new features. The new version will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date, and the link in the Game’s Settings always leads here. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will update this policy before the change takes effect in the Game.

15. Contact

Questions or requests about this privacy policy: stamgamesdev@gmail.com

This policy is available in several languages. If a translation differs from the English version, the English version applies.
