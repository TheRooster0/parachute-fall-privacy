Parachute Fall – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 September 2026 · Last updated: 2 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Parachute Fall (Android package name com.patpatt.game, the “Game”) handles information. The Game is developed and published by the developer named on the Game’s Google Play page (“we”, “us”, “our”). If you have any questions, contact us at stamgamesdev@gmail.com.

In short

  • The Game itself does not collect, sell or share any personal data.
  • There are no accounts or sign-ins, no advertisements, no analytics or tracking tools, and no in-app purchases.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your own device.
  • The Game needs an internet connection. It uses it only to check that the connection works and, if you take part in the follow reward, to look up whether your reward code has been confirmed. These requests contain no personal data.
  • Neither the Game nor this website uses cookies.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal data through the Game. The Game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it does not create or use advertising IDs or other device identifiers. If you take part in the Instagram follow reward, the limited processing described in section 5 applies.

2. Information stored only on your device

To remember your game, the Game saves a small file in its private storage on your device (Android “shared preferences”). It contains:

  • progress: unlocked levels and stars earned;
  • in-game items: gold, keys, abilities, and the parachutes and characters you have unlocked and equipped;
  • settings: sound, music, vibration, tilt control, graphics quality, control speed and language;
  • gameplay statistics, such as the number of levels completed and obstacles dodged;
  • the state of daily rewards and daily quests, whether the tutorial is finished and whether you have already been asked to rate the Game.
  • the follow reward: whether you have opened our Instagram page from the Game, the reward code the Game created for your device, whether you have sent it, and whether you have collected the reward.

The Game never sends this file off your device, and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Gold and keys in the Game have no real-world value.

3. Device features the Game uses

The Game uses the following features only on your device, while you play. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere:

  • Device language: read at first start to show the Game in your language (you can change it in Settings).
  • Date and time: used to unlock daily rewards and daily quests.
  • Touch screen and, only if you turn on Tilt Control in Settings, the motion sensor (accelerometer), to steer the parachutist.
  • Vibration: short vibrations as feedback; you can turn them off in Settings.

4. Permissions

The Game does not ask for any permission that needs your approval (such as location, camera, microphone, contacts, files or phone). Its Android package contains only permissions that are granted automatically at installation:

  • INTERNET: for the internet connection the Game needs to be played (see below).
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: to see whether your device is connected to a network, so that the Game can tell you at once when the connection is lost.
  • VIBRATE: for the vibration feedback described above.
  • An internal permission (com.patpatt.game.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION) that Android’s own libraries use to stop other apps from sending messages to the Game. It gives the Game access to nothing.

The Game is played online, but it uses the internet for only two things:

  • Connection check: while the Game is open, it downloads a tiny file (ping.txt) from our service on GitHub Pages (therooster0.github.io/parachute-fall-api) about every 30 seconds. If that file cannot be reached, it asks Google’s connection check (www.google.com/generate_204). Without a connection, the Game pauses until the connection is back.
  • Follow reward: when you tap VERIFY in the “Get a reward” window, the Game downloads a list of confirmed reward codes from the same service and looks for your code in it (see section 5). Your code is not sent; only the part of the list where your code would be is downloaded.

These requests contain no personal data and no account or device identifier. As with any internet connection, the servers that receive them (GitHub, and Google for the fallback check) see your IP address and basic technical information, such as the time of the request. Links you tap (for example, to rate the Game, to open Instagram or to read this policy or our Terms of Use) open in Google Play, the Instagram app or your web browser.

5. Third-party services

The Game contains no third-party advertising, analytics, social network or payment SDKs. However, the following services may process information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play: when you download, install or update the Game, Google processes information as described in the Google Privacy Policy. Through Google Play Console, Google gives us only aggregated statistics (for example, the number of installs) and, if you have allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics data, anonymous crash and performance reports that do not identify you.
  • Rating the Game: if you choose to rate the Game, it opens its page in the Google Play Store; ratings and reviews you post there are handled by Google.
  • Instagram (follow reward): if you tap FOLLOW in the “Get a reward” window, our Instagram page (@stamgamesdev) opens in the Instagram app or your browser. To confirm that you follow us, you can send the reward code shown in the Game to our account in an Instagram message. Our verification service then automatically reads the message through Meta’s Instagram API, uses the ID that Instagram gives the sender for our account to ask Instagram whether that account follows ours, and sends a short reply. It publishes only a one-way SHA-256 hash of a confirmed code, which cannot be linked to you or your Instagram account; it does not store your name, username, ID or message. Your message stays in our Instagram inbox like any other message; you can ask us to delete the conversation. Sending the code is your choice; the legal basis for this processing is your request to receive the reward (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Meta processes your use of Instagram as described in the Instagram Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub (our service): the connection check and the list of confirmed codes are hosted on GitHub Pages, and the verification service runs on GitHub Actions, both provided by GitHub, Inc. (USA). When the Game connects, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address (GitHub Privacy Statement).
  • Google connection check: only if our service cannot be reached, the Game asks www.google.com/generate_204, which returns an empty answer; as with any website, Google sees your IP address (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Unity: the Game is made with the Unity engine by Unity Technologies. We have turned off Unity’s optional data services (Unity Analytics, hardware statistics, crash and performance reporting) and left Unity’s analytics module out of the Game entirely, so the Game sends no data to Unity. More information: Unity Privacy Policy.
  • Android backup: if backup is turned on on your device, Android may include the Game’s saved data in your device backup stored in your Google Account. This backup is controlled by you and Google; we have no access to it.
  • This page: this privacy policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open it, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address (GitHub Privacy Statement). We do not add any cookies, analytics or tracking to this page.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

The Game does not use cookies, web beacons, advertising IDs, device fingerprinting or any similar tracking technology. The only thing it stores on your device is the save file described in section 2, which is strictly necessary for the Game to work and never leaves your device. This website (this policy and our Terms of Use) sets no cookies and loads nothing from other websites. That is why neither the Game nor this website shows a cookie consent banner: there is nothing to consent to.

7. Sharing and selling of data

Because the Game itself collects no personal data, we have nothing to sell, rent, trade or share with anyone, including advertisers and data brokers. The services described in sections 4 and 5 (Google, GitHub, Meta) are based in the United States and may process technical data, such as IP addresses, there under their own safeguards.

8. Advertising and in-app purchases

The Game shows no advertisements. Nothing can currently be bought with real money: shop items that would cost real money are marked “Soon”. Gold and keys earned in the Game have no real-world value. If we add features in the future that involve data (for example, in-app purchases whose payments would be processed by Google Play), we will update this policy before those features become available. For the rules on in-game items, purchases and refunds, see our Terms of Use.

9. Children’s privacy

The Game is suitable for a general audience, including children. Because the Game does not collect personal information from anyone, children can play it without providing any personal data. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the minimum age that applies in their country). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has sent us personal information (for example, by email), please contact us and we will delete it. The Instagram follow reward is meant for players who are old enough to use Instagram under its terms (at least 13).

10. If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We use them only to reply to you, do not share them with anyone, and delete them when they are no longer needed to handle your request.

11. Data retention and deletion

  • Data on your device: stays until you uninstall the Game or clear its data (Android Settings → Apps → Parachute Fall → Storage → Clear data). This permanently resets your progress.
  • Emails: kept only as long as needed to reply to you. You can ask us to delete them at any time.
  • Follow reward messages: the verification service reads new messages within minutes and keeps only the hash of a confirmed code and the time of the last message it read. Messages stay in our Instagram inbox until they are deleted; write to us if you would like us to delete yours.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) give you rights over your personal data, such as the right to access, correct, delete or port it, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw your consent. Because the Game does not collect personal data, these rights mainly concern emails you send us. To exercise them, write to stamgamesdev@gmail.com; we will reply within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority in your country. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA.

13. Security

Your game data stays in the Game’s private storage on your device, protected by Android’s app sandbox, and is not sent over the internet. The Game’s connection check and code lookup use encrypted HTTPS connections. Emails you send us are protected by the security measures of our email provider.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the Game gets new features. The new version will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date, and the link in the Game’s Settings always leads here. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will update this policy before the change takes effect in the Game.

15. Contact

Questions or requests about this privacy policy: stamgamesdev@gmail.com

This policy is available in several languages. If a translation differs from the English version, the English version applies.

Para recordar tu partida, el Juego guarda un pequeño archivo en su almacenamiento privado de tu dispositivo (“shared preferences” de Android). Contiene:

  • progreso: niveles desbloqueados y estrellas conseguidas;
  • objetos del juego: oro, llaves, habilidades y los paracaídas y personajes desbloqueados y equipados;
  • ajustes: sonido, música, vibración, control por inclinación, calidad gráfica, velocidad de control e idioma;
